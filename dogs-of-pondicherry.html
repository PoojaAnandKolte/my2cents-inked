Dogs of Pondicherry

Every paw has a place. Every encounter has a story.

A photographic journal of the dogs who share our streets, neighbourhoods and everyday lives.

Some are familiar faces. Some are chance encounters. Some have made a particular street corner their own. This is a growing collection of their photographs, little stories and places on the map.

Explore the map. Meet its residents.
